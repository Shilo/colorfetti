Caller trace: App imports `decodeImportedFile` lazily at file acquisition and re-prepares unpublished imports before batch publication; clear/source generations own cancellation. App Save/Copy/ZIP/hover captures settings and sources before awaiting; `still` uses whole-clip preparation then first-frame conversion. Tile independently subscribes to limiter/Cleanup with current-request cancellation. PaletteImport composes download timeout+dialog signal and rejects stale results. `palette-usage.js` shares export preparation but may use compact source representatives only for nearest; spatial conversions retain real pixels.

No performance claim follows from source review alone. Native/focused probes and measured decisions are separate receipts. No speculative production optimization proposed yet.
