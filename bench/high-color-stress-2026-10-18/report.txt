Assessment:
256 is a sensible existing ceiling for this app, whose color indexes 0–255
share an explicit empty sentinel of 256 and whose GIF exporter builds one shared
table. It is not a universal maximum for RGB images or artists' palette files.
Auto has a separate 64-color generation capacity. Output color count can also
be lower because of explicit Max colors settings or unused target colors.

No silent reduction, out-of-range wrapping, incomplete backup, lost transparency
or export color loss was reproduced in this audit. Two intentional UX limits:
oversized image/text drafts require manual color removal; oversized Lospec links
are rejected rather than opening the larger review draft. Neither increases the
active cap. The Lospec error describes the 1–256 requirement but does not offer
the image/text editor's remove-N workflow.
